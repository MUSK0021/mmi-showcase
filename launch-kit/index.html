    iiThe emails

    iiiThe checkout

    Three sensible ways to sell a PDF. Fees and features change, so confirm current terms before choosing.

    Recommended to start

    A hosted checkout, linked from the website

    Tools such as Gumroad, Stan Store or Lemon Squeezy host the checkout and send the PDF automatically. Add a button on the Showit site and a link in the Instagram bio. Gumroad and Lemon Squeezy also act as merchant of record, collecting and paying sales tax for you.

    Best for the first guide.

    When the range grows

    A Shopify store

    A home for several guides, templates or printed goods. Shopify’s Digital Downloads app delivers the files, and the store can sit on its own subdomain of the website.

    Best from three products onwards.

    For reach

    An Etsy listing

    A marketplace where buyers already search for wedding guides and planning resources. The trade-off is less control over branding and the customer relationship.

    Best as a second channel, not the first.

    Start with one guide, one button and one checkout. Move to a full store once there are three or more products to sell.

    Before the first sale
    • Final PDF proofread, exported and compressed for email delivery
    • Cover image and three preview spreads
    • Product description and who it’s for
    • Founding price, full price and the date the founding price ends
    • Automatic delivery tested with a real purchase
    • Receipt email written in the studio’s voice
    • Terms: personal use only, no sharing or resale
    • A clear refund policy for digital products
    • Waitlist and buyers tagged in the email tool
    • Sales tax settings checked
    • A thank-you page with one next step
